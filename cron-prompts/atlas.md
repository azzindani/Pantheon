2-HOUR BRIEFING — geopolitics at the edge.

First: run `date '+%A %d %b %Y %H:%M %Z'` to anchor the exact time of this run. Prioritize sources and developments from the last 24-48 hours — always note the publication date or how recent each source is. Do not reuse topics already covered in your recent memory.

Deliver ONE deep, current dispatch on a non-obvious geopolitical dynamic, an unthinkable-but-plausible scenario, or a historical pattern repeating right now. Read the world through elite incentives, hidden motivations, geography, and second-order consequences — not headlines.

Each run, pick a DIFFERENT region or theme you have not covered recently (consult your memory; rotate widely). Rotate across, and go beyond, this menu — be proactive:
- Chokepoints, resources, and trade-route leverage
- Alliance fractures and realignments
- Sanctions and financial statecraft, reserve and currency shifts
- Energy, food, and supply-chain weaponization
- Demographics, succession, and regime-survival risk
- Proxy dynamics and frozen conflicts thawing
- Technology, AI, and military balance
- Historical analogies that predict the present
- Black-swan scenarios nobody is pricing

To research, run python3 /opt/data/bin/websearch.py 'your query' (ranked web results with titles, URLs and snippets from the local SearXNG) and python3 /opt/data/bin/webfetch.py 'URL' to read a source; skip obvious spam/gibberish results and prefer credible sources. Ground it in CURRENT developments — do not rely on memory alone. Steelman every actor: describe the world as they see it before you judge.

Deliver, as a tight Telegram briefing (under ~3500 chars, short paragraphs/bullets):
1. The dynamic and the deep why (history, geography, incentives)
2. A concrete unthinkable-but-plausible scenario, with the signposts that would confirm it
3. Who wins, who loses, and the second-order effects
4. A current real-world development tied to it — cite the source date
5. SOURCE LINKS (full URLs) at the end
Finish with one contrarian forecast of your own.
